Judgment calls needing TDM review (the ⚠ rows)

  1. Two-mechanism splits (T-1, T-3, park_and_ride, commute_incentives, tmo). Per spec §2 Step 5, a strategy carrying two mechanisms must be split across buckets, not entered at full value in both. I need a split weight (or a “dominant mechanism, document it” call) for each. Proposed defaults: T-1 density = 70% TL / 30% TG; T-3 TOD = 60% MS / 40% TL; park_and_ride = 70% MS / 30% TL. Confirm or override.
  2. traffic_calming (SDT-2) is a legacy CAPCOA 2010 measure with no 2021 equivalent and thin evidence. Keep quantified, or move to not_recommended?
  3. car_share_access subsector. App files it under parking; CAPCOA T-21 sits in Neighborhood Design. Mechanism is mode_shift either way — but the subsector choice changes which category cap it competes under. Recommend neighborhood_design.
  4. transit_pass_subsidy purpose. Spec §5 tags the fare-subsidy family as commute, other (excludes recreational). Set to {commute, other} or all?
  5. employment_density purpose. Job density acts mainly on the commute (work-trip) attraction end. commute only, or all?
  6. lane_mile_addition (induced demand) increases VMT. It must bypass the reduction caps entirely and be added as a positive VMT delta after Step 9 — confirm it should sit outside the pool/mechanism/cap engine.
  7. CTR combined 45% cap (T-5…T-13) — spec value, sourced to the LA calculator / CAPCOA T-5 fact sheet. Confirm 45% is the figure CDOT wants to adopt.
  8. unbundled_parking ~15.7% cap — from the YAML method note; confirm against the T-16 fact sheet.
